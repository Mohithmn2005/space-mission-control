🌍 NASA Destination Science Data

Explore real science information about the destinations available in this educational mission simulator.

🌙 THE MOON

Average distance from Earth: About 384,400 km.

Radius: About 1,740 km.

Surface: A rocky world covered with craters, mountains and fine lunar dust.

Water: Water ice is present in some permanently shadowed regions near the lunar poles.

Environment: The Moon has almost no atmosphere and experiences extreme temperature changes.

Mission challenge: Spacecraft must consider landing terrain, sunlight, temperature and communication.

Read NASA Moon Facts ↗

🔴 MARS

Average distance from the Sun: About 228 million km.

Length of a day: About 24.6 Earth hours.

Length of a year: About 687 Earth days.

Atmosphere: Very thin and composed mostly of carbon dioxide.

Surface: A cold desert with volcanoes, valleys, craters and evidence of ancient flowing water.

Mission challenge: Dust storms, cold temperatures, communication delays and safe landing.

Read NASA Mars Facts ↗

🪐 EUROPA — JUPITER'S MOON

Location: Europa is one of the major moons of Jupiter.

Average distance from Jupiter: About 671,000 km.

Surface: An icy crust marked by cracks, ridges and bands.

Ocean: Evidence suggests a salty liquid-water ocean beneath its icy shell.

Environment: Europa experiences intense radiation from Jupiter's magnetic environment.

Mission challenge: Radiation protection, remote observations and studying the icy surface.
